Honest advice. Careful work. One team.

Our story

We started SK Energy because too many homeowners were being sold packages, not systems designed for their property.

Every job starts with a proper survey and an honest conversation — including telling you when something isn’t worth doing yet. Our own accredited engineers carry out every installation, and the same team stays with you for servicing and support.
